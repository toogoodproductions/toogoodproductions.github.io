Questions, answered straight.

What is AI video production?

AI video production means the film is generated rather than filmed. No crew, no location, no shoot day. The script, direction and edit still happen the way they always have. Only the footage is made differently, which is what makes it faster and cheaper than a conventional shoot.

Will an AI video look artificial?

Not if it's made properly. The tell in most AI video comes from rushing: faces that shift between shots, hands that go wrong, movement that reads slightly off. Those are known failure points and they're avoidable. We build around them rather than hoping nobody notices.

How is this different from a traditional shoot?

You skip everything that costs time and money before the camera rolls. No location scouting, no casting, no crew, no shoot day, no weather. What you keep is the writing, the direction and the edit. That's why an AI film lands in days rather than weeks.

How long does it take?

Days rather than months. Because there's no shoot to schedule, most of the timeline is decision-making: agreeing the idea, approving the storyboard, then the edit. Exact timing depends on length and complexity, and you get a date before we start.

What does it cost?

Less than the same film would cost as a shoot, because the crew, location and shoot day come out of the budget entirely. Price depends on length, how many scenes, and how much of it involves people on screen. We quote per project before any work starts.

Can you make several versions of the same ad?

Yes, and it's one of the strongest reasons to use AI. Testing ten hooks for the same Meta ad is impossible with a shoot and straightforward with a generated one. You get the variations you need to find what actually performs.

What kinds of video do you make?

Ad films, brand films, social reels, launch videos, product films, AI avatars and animated films. If it plays on a screen, we make it. We also build the systems that let a brand produce this kind of work continuously instead of one film at a time.

Do you work with clients outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. We're based in Ahmedabad and work with brands across India and outside it. Because there's no shoot, there's nothing to travel to. The whole process runs remotely: brief, storyboard, review, delivery.

Which industries do you work with?

Real estate, healthcare, manufacturing and consumer brands so far. The work translates anywhere a business needs to show something expensive, difficult or impossible to film, which covers most product launches and almost all property.

How do we start?

Tell us what you're making and what it needs to do. We come back with the idea, the timeline and the price. Nothing gets built until you've approved the storyboard.
